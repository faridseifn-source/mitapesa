1. Who we are

MitaPesa is a personal finance and mobile payments application operated by Duga Pay ("Duga Pay," "we," "us," or "our"), based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the MitaPesa mobile application and website (together, the "Service"), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights available to you.

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Policy.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: first name, last name, email address, mobile phone number, and password.
  • Identity verification (KYC) information: for transactions above our verification threshold, we collect and verify your National Identification Authority (NIDA) number and associated record details to confirm your identity, as required by Tanzanian financial regulations. This may include a one-time verification code sent to your registered number, or answers to security questions drawn from your NIDA record.
  • Transaction and financial information: transaction amounts, categories, merchants, dates, notes, and receipt photos you choose to add; budget limits you set; bank account and card details you provide when adding a top-up method or transferring funds.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting support or submitting a dispute, complaint, or inquiry.
  • Profile photo: an optional photo you choose to upload.

Biometric login (Face ID / fingerprint):

If you choose to enable Face ID or fingerprint login, MitaPesa uses your device's built-in biometric authentication (WebAuthn/passkey technology) rather than storing or processing your biometric data ourselves.

  • Your fingerprint or facial data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers. It is processed entirely by your device's own operating system and secure hardware.
  • What we store on our servers is a cryptographic public key associated with your device, used to verify sign-in attempts. This key cannot be used to reconstruct your fingerprint or face.
  • You can remove any enrolled device from your account at any time from the app's settings.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and technical information: device type, operating system, app version, and a push notification token used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Usage information: how you interact with the Service, for app functionality and security purposes.

Information from third parties:

  • Where you enable transactions through payment rails such as TIPS, GePG, LUKU, or partner banks, we receive transaction confirmations and related details necessary to process your request.
  • If you use receipt scanning, the photo you provide is processed by a third-party image-recognition service solely to extract the merchant, amount, and category.
  • If you use voice expense logging, your spoken recording is sent to a third-party AI service solely to convert it to text and identify expense details (amount, merchant, category, and date); the recording is not stored on our own servers at any point, and is discarded immediately after processing.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your account, and authenticate you when you log in;
  • Process transactions and payments you request, including top-ups, transfers, bill payments, and merchant payments;
  • Verify your identity where required by law, and manage risk-based authentication for larger transactions;
  • Provide budgeting, categorization, and spending insight features;
  • Send you transactional notifications and, where enabled, other push notifications;
  • Respond to support requests, disputes, and complaints;
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, unauthorized transactions, and other illegal activity;
  • Comply with applicable financial services, anti-money-laundering, and identity-verification laws and regulations;
  • Improve and maintain the security and reliability of the Service.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your financial or identity data for targeted advertising.

4. How we share your information

We share information only as necessary to provide the Service, and in the following circumstances:

  • Payment and banking partners: with TIPS, GePG, LUKU, card processors, and partner banks, as needed to route and complete transactions you initiate.
  • Service providers: with vendors who help us operate the Service, such as cloud hosting, push notification delivery, and AI-based processing for receipt scanning and voice expense logging — under contractual obligations to protect your information.
  • Identity verification: with NIDA, solely to confirm your identity where required.
  • Shared wallets: if you choose to create or join a Shared Household Wallet, other members can see transactions logged to that wallet, including who logged them — a feature you and other members explicitly opt into.
  • Legal and regulatory requirements: where required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or request from a competent authority in Tanzania.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections.

5. Data retention

We retain your personal and transaction information for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as necessary to comply with our legal obligations (including financial record-keeping requirements under Tanzanian law), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Identity verification records are retained in accordance with applicable anti-money-laundering regulations.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information — you can update your name directly from your Profile settings, and can contact support for other corrections;
  • Export your data — you can download a CSV export of your transaction history at any time from the Insights section of the app;
  • Delete your account and associated personal data, subject to our obligation to retain certain financial records as required by law;
  • Withdraw consent for optional features, such as push notifications or biometric login, at any time from Settings;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit, secure storage of credentials, and access controls limiting who within our organization can view sensitive information. Full card numbers and security codes are never stored in a readable form and are only retrieved momentarily, on request, when you choose to view them.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

8. Children's privacy

MitaPesa is not directed to, and is not intended for use by, individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or by other reasonable means before the changes take effect. The date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last revised.

10. Governing law and contact us

This Policy is governed by the laws of the United Republic of Tanzania, including the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.
