Delete your MitaPesa account

MitaPesa is a personal finance and payments app operated by Duga Pay  ·  Last updated: October 9, 2026

Option 1 — in the app (fastest)

  1. Open MitaPesa and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account (Futa akaunti in Swahili).
  4. Enter your password, optionally tell us why you are leaving, tick the confirmation box, and tap Delete my account.

You will see a confirmation on screen and receive an email confirming that your account was deleted.

Option 2 — if you cannot use the app

If you have uninstalled the app, changed phone, or cannot sign in, you can ask us to delete your account without the app:

  1. Email info@dugapay.com from the email address registered on your MitaPesa account, with the subject Delete my MitaPesa account.
  2. Include the mobile phone number on the account.
  3. We will reply to confirm that the request is really from you, for example by asking you to confirm a one-time code sent to your registered email address. We will never ask for your password.

We acknowledge requests within 3 working days and complete verified requests within 30 days. If you have simply forgotten your password, you can reset it from the sign-in screen and then use Option 1.

Before you delete

  • If a MitaPesa card of yours still holds money, your account cannot be deleted straight away. We lock the card, send a closure request to our card partner, and notify you when the balance has been settled. You can then delete your account.
  • If you own a shared household wallet with other members, remove the other members first.

What happens to your data

Removed from the app straight away:

  • Your expenses, receipt photos, budgets, and categories
  • Your membership of shared wallets, your saved devices, and your notifications
  • Your ability to sign in. Your email address and phone number can be used to register again.

Held for a limited period, then erased: we keep one restricted, secure copy of the information above for 90 days, so that we can restore your account if you change your mind or help resolve a dispute. Only authorised staff can open it, and access is logged. After 90 days it is permanently erased.

Records we are required to keep. If you have used a MitaPesa card, completed an identity check, or made payments, Tanzanian anti-money-laundering and financial record-keeping rules require us to keep your identity details (your name, contact details, and the details confirmed by your identity check) and your card and transaction records for at least 10 years after you close your account. They are stored securely, used only for legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution purposes, and never for marketing. We may keep them longer where a law, court order, or competent authority requires it.

If you have only used the budgeting features and never a card, an identity check, or payments, none of this applies: once the 90 days have passed, all of your personal information is erased. Deleted information may remain in routine backups for a short time until they are overwritten.
